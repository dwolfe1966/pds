Austin, TX Area Profile

Founded 1835Travis CountyElev. 149 m

Austin is home to roughly 967,862 residents. Austin was founded in 1835 and sits in Travis County, and is nicknamed "City of the Violet Crown". The median age is 34.5 and the median household income is $91,461. Homes carry a median value of $512,700, and about 44% of residents own where they live. White residents make up the largest share of the population (47.6%). Around 58% of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher, and the typical commute runs about 24 minutes.
