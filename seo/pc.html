Houston, TX Area Profile

Founded 1836Harris CountyElev. 13 m

Houston is a city in Texas with about 2,300,419 residents. Houston was founded in 1836 and sits in Harris County, and is nicknamed "Space City". The median age is 34.3 and the median household income is $62,894. Homes carry a median value of $253,400, and about 42% of residents own where they live. Hispanic or Latino residents make up the largest share of the population (44.1%). Around 36% of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher, and the typical commute runs about 27 minutes.
