Overview

About Jacksonville

Jacksonville is home to roughly 961,739 residents. Jacksonville was founded in 1791 and sits in Duval County. The median age is 36.4 and the median household income is $66,981. Homes carry a median value of $266,100, and about 57% of residents own where they live. White residents make up the largest share of the population (47.7%). Around 32% of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher, and the typical commute runs about 24 minutes.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (5-year).
