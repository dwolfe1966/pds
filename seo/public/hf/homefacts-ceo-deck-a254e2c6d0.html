What HomeFacts actually captures

High-intent people searches — the most valuable traffic online.

Beneath the neighborhood-data skin, HomeFacts' top organic queries are people-intent — someone checking a person, prompted by an address or a safety concern:

Sex-offender registry lookups Person-name searches Neighborhood-safety checks Background & public-records intent "Who lives at this address"

This is the exact audience that converts on identity and background products — a commercially premium intent that HomeFacts owns organically and, today, hands away.

03

The problem

Its reach is slipping — and its best intent is monetized once.

On a 2013 stack no one can act on, HomeFacts' organic reach — its share of search impressions — erodes year over year. And its most valuable traffic, high-intent people searches, is monetized today as a one-time impression — not an ongoing customer relationship.

HomeFacts search → Monetized as display, today → Value captured once

This traffic is HomeFacts' to redirect at any time. The value isn't gone — the reach can be rebuilt, and that intent can become customers you keep a share of.

04

The proposal

Keep the customer. Share the lifetime value.

Route that same high-intent traffic into IDLookup's own, modern people-search funnel and billing. Instead of a one-time payout, NIC shares in the lifetime value of every IDLookup subscription generated from HomeFacts traffic.

HomeFacts search → IDLookup owned funnel & billing → NIC shares subscription LTV — recurring
  • ✓Recurring revenue from the identical clicks — a share of ongoing subscription LTV, not a one-time payout.
  • ✓Zero cost, zero effort — and you never touch the codebase again. The reason HomeFacts feels stuck is the code you can't modify. We take that off your plate entirely: we run and rebuild it on infrastructure we already operate.
  • ✓NIC keeps 100% of its display-ad business — different intent, same pages, no cannibalization. And because we rebuild the SEO, we grow the reach — and the search impressions — those ads are sold against.
  • ✓Your SEO equity is preserved — no rip-and-replace. The modern experience runs at HomeFacts' existing URLs; we change what's behind each page, not its address. No mass redirects, no ranking risk.
  • ✓Ownership, brand & control stay with NIC. HomeFacts remains HomeFacts.
