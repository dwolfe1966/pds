Overview

About Miami

With about 446,663 residents, Miami is one of Florida's notable places. Miami was founded in 1825 and sits in Miami-Dade County, and is nicknamed "The Magic City". The median age is 39.7 and the median household income is $59,390. Homes carry a median value of $475,200, and about 31% of residents own where they live. Hispanic or Latino residents make up the largest share of the population (71.2%). Around 36% of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher, and the typical commute runs about 27 minutes.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (5-year).
