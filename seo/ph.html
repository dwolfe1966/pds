Overview

About Phoenix

With about 1,624,832 residents, Phoenix is one of Arizona's notable places. Phoenix was founded in 1868 and sits in Maricopa County, and is nicknamed "Valley of the Sun". The median age is 34.8 and the median household income is $77,041. Homes carry a median value of $381,900, and about 57% of residents own where they live. Hispanic or Latino residents make up the largest share of the population (41.8%). Around 32% of adults hold a bachelor's degree or higher, and the typical commute runs about 26 minutes.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey (5-year).
