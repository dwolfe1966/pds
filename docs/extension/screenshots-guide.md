# Chrome Web Store screenshots — IDLookup Remove

Practical guide for capturing the store listing screenshots.

## Requirements (Chrome Web Store)

- **Minimum: 1 screenshot** (5 recommended for a strong listing).
- **Dimensions: 1280×800** (preferred) **or 640×400**. No other sizes are accepted.
- **Format: PNG (24-bit, no alpha) or JPEG.**
- Fill the whole frame — no letterboxing, no rounded corners, no drop shadows baked in.
- Capture at 100% zoom on a Retina display, then downscale to exact pixels so text stays crisp.

## The 3 recommended shots

1. **Assist panel on a real broker page.**
   Open a live opt-out page (e.g. `thatsthem.com/optout`) with the IDLookup assist panel docked and the **"Autofill" button clearly visible**. This is the money shot — it shows the core job the extension does.

2. **"Your Digital Footprint" view.**
   Show the protection score and the category map (where the person appears across brokers). Communicates the ongoing value beyond a single form.

3. **Popup with browsing-insights toggle OFF.**
   Show the extension popup with the optional monitoring / browsing-insights toggle in the **OFF** state. Signals that monitoring is opt-in and user-controlled — reinforces the honest, consent-first positioning.

## Framing tips

- Target **1280×800**. Capture the browser window (or a clean crop of it) and resize to exact pixels.
- Leave a little breathing room around the panel; don't crop the CTA button or score.
- Use a real, recognizable broker page so reviewers see the extension in genuine context.
- Consistent light theme across all shots; hide unrelated toolbars/bookmarks bars.
- Optional: a short caption band (brand green `#0d5d2f`, white text) along the top or bottom to label each shot — keep it minimal.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Use placeholder identity data: a name + city is fine (e.g. "Jordan Rivera, Austin TX").
- Show the actual UI, current build, real broker domains.
- Keep every screenshot at exactly 1280×800 or 640×400.

**Don't**
- Never show a real **SSN, date of birth, full address, phone, email, or card number** — not even blurred. Name + city only.
- No fake/mocked screens that don't match the shipped UI.
- No competitor logos, no stock-photo people, no marketing claims the extension doesn't deliver.
- Don't upscale a small capture to hit 1280×800 (it'll look soft) — capture large, downscale.
