I'm David Wolfe, CEO of People Data Systems — the company behind IDLookup, a people-search platform. Tim Chin suggested I reach out and connect with you directly (cc'd here).

Turning high-intent organic search traffic into a recurring consumer business is the pattern I've built my career on. Before IDLookup, I was CPO at MyLife, where I built the SEO strategy that grew it to ~20M monthly Google visitors (2014–2018).

I've been studying HomeFacts, and I think it's misunderstood as a cost.

It's a genuinely authoritative domain — by its own media kit, ~2.5M visits a month, though independent estimates show the organic search that drives it sliding for years. Its highest-value traffic — the person-name and safety searches — is handed to an outside affiliate for a per-lead fee, while the operator on the other end keeps the customer and all the lifetime value. The value isn't gone; it's stuck, because no one can act on the site.

So I rebuilt it, to show what's possible: idlookup.me/homefacts. Every HomeFacts module, modernized — plus a dozen it doesn't have, and the one thing property data can't answer: who lives here.

The idea: my team runs and rebuilds HomeFacts' SEO and converts the person-search traffic through our own people-search funnel — you earn a share of the customer lifetime value it produces. You never touch the codebase and take on zero cost. Your ad business is untouched; if anything, rebuilding the SEO grows the traffic your display ads are sold against.

Worth 20 minutes? I'd be glad to walk you through it.
