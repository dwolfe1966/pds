## From affiliate broker to an owned growth engine

- Underneath the real-estate skin, **HomeFacts is a people-search traffic engine**.
- Today it **hands that traffic to TruthFinder & Intelius** for a thin affiliate fee — while its own SEO declines.
- **Proposal:** IDLookup runs HomeFacts's SEO (up to a full rebuild) *and* converts that traffic through our own owned funnel.

> Speaker note: The pitch stands on strategic value + economics. Don't lean on any ownership/investor tie.

---

## 01 · The proposal

- HomeFacts's biggest organic traffic is people-search intent (neighborhood-safety / sex-offender / person-name).
- Today it's handed to Intelius & TruthFinder for a thin fee; the site's SEO is declining.
- **We propose to reverse both** — revive the SEO, convert the traffic through IDLookup's own funnel → capture full value, not a per-lead sliver.
- Low commitment to start: **a pilot** that proves the economics on real traffic.

> Speaker note: The funnels are built; the switch isn't on yet — turning it on is part of the ask. The affiliate is non-exclusive, so the traffic is HomeFacts's to redirect.

---

## 02 · The asset — and the reality

- **Authoritative:** **Moz DA 47**, ~2,100 referring domains, **est. 1996 (~30-yr domain)** — real, hard-won authority.
- **Declining:** **~135K visits/mo and falling** (SimilarWeb '26), ~71% organic; **10.3M indexed pages** on a **2013-era stack (PHP 5.3)**.
- **Engine = people-search, not property** — top queries are person names + "sex offender"; audience overlaps Intelius/TruthFinder, not Zillow.
- **Best intent brokered away** — CAKE redirect to TruthFinder & Intelius (PeopleConnect, non-exclusive), per-lead fee, name passed through. Verified live 2026-07-29.

> Speaker note: The punchline — an authoritative people-search domain, declining, renting its best traffic away for pennies. Owned by Nations Info Corp since 2022 (from ATTOM/RealtyTrac); a real asset without a gardener.

---

## 03 · The #1 lever — own the funnel

- **Today:** HomeFacts → TruthFinder/Intelius. Per-lead sliver; the competitor keeps the signup + lifetime value.
- **Proposed:** HomeFacts → IDLookup's owned funnel + billing. NIC shares in **full customer lifetime value** — the whole economics of the same click.
- Same clicks, materially more revenue. Funnel already built; the pilot switches it on.

> Speaker note: Single biggest value lever. LTV share > affiliate check on identical traffic.

---

## 04 · Four compounding wins

- **A · Lever** — capture the LTV you're renting away (owned funnel vs. affiliate payout).
- **B · Engine** — reverse the decline via SEO turnaround on the existing URL architecture (low structural risk).
- **C · Greenfield** — monetize the address→person intent on property pages (display-only today).
- **D · Moat** — HomeFacts's property graph × IDLookup's people/incarceration graph, interlinked → compounding SEO + a shared data asset.

> Speaker note: Emphasize **non-cannibalizing** — real-estate/mortgage and people-search convert different intents on the same pages. Additive.

---

## 05 · The combined value — place × person

- **HomeFacts knows the PLACE** — property, neighborhood, crime, schools, hazards, offender geography (100M+ homes).
- **IDLookup knows the PERSON** — identity, contact, relatives, first-party incarceration, life-events (214M+ profiles) + owned funnel.
- **Natural bridge:** address → who lives here → the person. Two audiences cross-pollinate; two monetization lanes, same pages.
- **The ambition:** don't bolt on a funnel — **rebuild HomeFacts** on modern infra, merge both graphs, and make it a category-leading place-and-person destination.

> Speaker note: This is the "sell the synergies" slide + the rebuild vision. 1 + 1 = 3.

## 07 · Why NIC says yes

- **Zero cost, zero effort** — untouched, HomeFacts only declines.
- **More money from the same traffic** — LTV share beats the affiliate fee, plus growth.
- **100% of real-estate value stays NIC's** — and a healthier HomeFacts strengthens NIC's property-search position (vs. Zillow et al.).

## 08 · The model — clear lanes, no overlap

| | **IDLookup** | **NIC / HomeFacts** |
|---|---|---|
| **Runs** | SEO · maintenance/rebuild · content · people-search funnel + billing · reporting | Ownership · brand · real-estate / mortgage & ad monetization |
| **Keeps** | People-search revenue (rev-share to NIC) | 100% of real-estate value; all upside on grown traffic |
| **Guardrails** | No brand/structural change without sign-off; FCRA maintained | Access to code/CMS; a lightweight pilot |

- **Revenue share: kept open** — structured on the pilot's measured results.

> Speaker note: Flexible split lowers the barrier to "yes."

## 09 · How it rolls out — prove on a pilot, then compound

- **Wk 2–4** — Access, audit & baseline (access to how the code is managed is already in motion).
- **Wk 4–8** — SEO triage + owned-funnel pilot on top placements.
- **Mo 2–3** — Platform migration (staged), URL/301 integrity per slice.
- **Mo 3–4** — Data-merge v1 + address→person vertical; content-quality pass.
- **Mo 4–6+** — Full re-platform & compound; grow both lanes; ROI vs baseline.

> Speaker note: Reversible at each step. Phase 0 is literally the question David is already asking.

## 10 · The rebuild — six workstreams

- **Platform** — off the 2013 stack, preserving URL/301 equity.
- **SEO** — triage → content quality.
- **Data** — merge place × person; address→resident→person.
- **Product** — modernize + embed owned funnel + open address→person vertical.
- **Monetization** — two lanes; retire the broker.
- **Measurement** — baseline → prove it (traffic, indexation, conversion, real-estate demand, revenue by lane).

> Speaker note: Makes "rebuild" concrete and credible, not just a vision line.
