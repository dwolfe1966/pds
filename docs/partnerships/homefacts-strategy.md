# HomeFacts × IDLookup — From Affiliate Broker to Owned Growth Engine

**Initial thoughts for Nations Info Corp (David), prepared for the partnership conversation.** Draft — owner review before sharing. Third-party traffic/SEO figures are public-tool estimates (order-of-magnitude).

---

## TL;DR — the proposal in one breath

HomeFacts is, underneath the real-estate skin, a **people-search traffic engine** — and today it **rents that traffic to TruthFinder and Intelius** for a thin affiliate fee while the site's own SEO quietly declines. **IDLookup takes over HomeFacts's SEO and replaces the competitor brokerage with our own owned people-search funnel** — reversing the traffic decline *and* capturing full customer value instead of a per-lead payout. NIC keeps 100% of its real-estate/property monetization (different intent, same pages), takes on **zero cost and zero effort**, and earns a revenue share that beats the affiliate check. Because David sits on **both** sides — as HomeFacts's owner and as a major investor in IDLookup — the same effort compounds twice.

We're not proposing a new relationship. We're proposing to **10× one that already works.**

---

## 1 · Where we are today

HomeFacts already sends IDLookup its records-intent outbound (sex-offender / background / public-records CTAs), pre-filled with name + city + state — a ~**70k-visitor** test that **displaced the old Intelius/TruthFinder handoff** on those placements. The plumbing, attribution (by placement), and partnership are **live**. This is about turning that passive tap into a jointly-built engine.

## 2 · The asset — and the reality

HomeFacts is a RealtyTrac-heritage neighborhood-due-diligence site (property, crime, **sex-offender registry**, schools, environmental & natural-hazard risk) across 100M+ homes, on an established domain with a huge indexed footprint (**~10.3M pages**). Owned by **Nations Info Corp** since 2022 (acquired from ATTOM/RealtyTrac).

But the numbers tell the real story:
- **~135K visits/mo and declining (≈ −18% MoM),** ~71–87% from organic search. A depreciating asset.
- **Its traffic engine is people-search, not property** — top organic queries are person names + "sex offender," and its audience cluster overlaps **Intelius, TruthFinder, TruePeopleSearch** — not Zillow.
- **Aging stack** (PHP 5.3, ~2013-era) with a *freshly-tended* SEO layer (recently-curated robots.txt) — i.e., **under-invested, not abandoned**: the scaffolding is there, the investment isn't.
- **It monetizes its best (people-search) intent by brokering it away** — a CAKE affiliate redirector hands high-intent name/address searches to TruthFinder & Intelius. HomeFacts earns a per-lead fee; the competitor keeps the customer and the LTV.

**An authoritative people-search domain, declining, renting its best traffic to our competitors. That's the opportunity.**

## 3 · The strategic thesis — four compounding wins

**A. Capture the LTV you're renting away — the #1 lever.**
HomeFacts today gets an affiliate *sliver* of a customer it hands to TruthFinder/Intelius. IDLookup is a real people-search operator with our own funnel and billing. Route that same high-intent traffic into **our owned funnel** and NIC shares in **full customer lifetime value**, not a per-lead payout — materially more revenue from the *exact same clicks*, and it stops feeding direct competitors.

**B. Reverse the traffic decline — SEO revival.**
A −18%/mo slide on a 10.3M-page domain with an aging stack is a classic, high-leverage SEO turnaround — same-URL-architecture rebuild, low structural risk. We just executed this playbook on our own directory. And see "Why us" — this is precisely the scale problem we've solved before.

**C. Monetize the address→person greenfield.**
HomeFacts's property/address pages carry "who owns / lives here" intent but today run **only display ads** — no people-search handoff at all. That's unmonetized upside a people-search partner is purpose-built to convert, on top of the existing traffic base.

**D. Authority + data flywheel — the moat.**
HomeFacts's property/address graph and IDLookup's people / identity / incarceration / life-events graph are **complementary**. Interlinked on an authoritative domain, they compound each other's SEO and build a richer, jointly-valuable data asset over time. Neither of us builds this alone.

**Crucially, none of this cannibalizes NIC.** Real-estate/property/mortgage (Bankrate, display, listings) and people-search monetize **different intents on the same pages** — genuinely additive.

## 4 · Why us

- **David Wolfe personally built people-search SEO at exactly this scale.** He led the SEO platform that drove **MyLife.com to ~20M monthly unique visitors from Google and 4M+ from Bing (2014–2018)** — the precise discipline (long-tail person/geo directory SEO) HomeFacts needs, proven at 100× HomeFacts's current traffic.
- **We just rebuilt our own directory's SEO** end-to-end (indexation, sitemap architecture, redirect recovery) — the muscle is warm.
- **We're a real people-search operator**, not an affiliate — owned funnel, billing, first-party data (incl. nationwide first-party incarceration data). We can capture LTV HomeFacts currently gives away.
- **We're already the traffic partner**, and **David wins on both sides** — the same effort grows HomeFacts *and* his IDLookup investment.

## 5 · Why NIC says yes (their side)

- **Zero cost, zero effort** — NIC has no SEO/engineering for HomeFacts; untouched it only declines. We take the whole burden.
- **More money from the same traffic** — LTV share > affiliate fee, plus growth on top.
- **100% of real-estate value stays NIC's** — we don't touch property/mortgage monetization.
- **Ownership, brand, and control stay NIC's** — no structural/brand changes without sign-off; prove it on a pilot first.

## 6 · The model (who does what)

| | IDLookup | NIC / HomeFacts |
|---|---|---|
| **Runs** | SEO · tech maintenance · content · people-search funnel + billing · reporting | Ownership · brand · real-estate/property + ad/mortgage monetization |
| **Keeps** | People-search revenue (rev-share to NIC) | 100% of real-estate/property value; all upside on grown traffic |
| **Guardrails** | No brand/structural change without NIC sign-off; FCRA/compliance maintained | Access to code/CMS; a lightweight pilot |

**Revenue share: kept flexible / open** — structured on the pilot's measured results (net people-search revenue share, tiered on growth, whatever reads cleanest to David). The point today is the *direction*, not the split.

## 7 · Phased plan

- **Phase 0 — Access & baseline (2–4 wks).** Access to how the site/code is managed (already in motion — "how the code is managed"); full SEO + traffic + monetization audit; baseline.
- **Phase 1 — Stop the bleeding (4–8 wks).** Technical SEO triage on the existing URL architecture — the recovery we just ran on our own directory. Begin swapping the affiliate broker for our owned funnel on the highest-intent placements.
- **Phase 2 — Grow & integrate (2–4 mo).** Content expansion on high-intent queries; open the address→person vertical; optimize the owned funnel; lift real-estate demand capture.
- **Phase 3 — Compound (ongoing).** Scale content + data interlinking; expand both monetization lanes; ROI reporting to both sides.

## 8 · What we need to start

Access to **how the code / site is managed** (the question already in motion) + a **lightweight pilot agreement.** Low commitment, fast proof.

## 9 · The upside — beyond "validation"

We first framed HomeFacts as a validation channel. It's bigger: a **controlled, authoritative people-search growth asset we compound together** — and a template for other under-invested NIC digital properties (RealtyStore, etc.). For David it's rare alignment: one effort that revives HomeFacts, stops funding our competitors, and grows the IDLookup investment at the same time.
