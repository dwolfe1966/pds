# HomeFacts × IDLookup — From Traffic Partner to Strategic Growth Engine

**Initial thoughts for NIC (David), prepared for the partnership conversation.** Draft — owner review before sharing.

---

## TL;DR — the proposal in one breath

**IDLookup takes over HomeFacts's SEO, technical maintenance, and content growth.** NIC gets a revived, growing, dual-monetized asset — property/real-estate demand *plus* a people-search revenue share — at **zero cost and zero effort**. IDLookup gets a high-authority traffic engine for people search. Because David sits on **both** sides of this — as HomeFacts's owner and as a major investor in IDLookup — value compounds on both.

We're not proposing a new relationship. We're proposing to **10× one that already works.**

---

## 1 · Where we are today

HomeFacts is already an IDLookup traffic partner. Its records-intent outbound (sex-offender, background-check, public-records CTAs on its neighborhood pages) now flows to **our** funnels — pre-filled with name + city + state — replacing the old Intelius / TruthFinder handoff. Current test scale ≈ **70k visitors**, attributed by placement.

**So the plumbing, the attribution, and the partnership are live.** This conversation is about turning a passive referral tap into a growth engine we jointly build.

## 2 · The asset — and the problem

HomeFacts is a genuinely valuable property-intelligence site with RealtyTrac heritage: property records, neighborhood data, crime, sex-offender maps, schools, and environmental/natural hazards — the exact "should I live here / who lives here" content people search for. It sits on an **established, authoritative domain** with years of accumulated trust and backlinks.

But since the RealtyTrac deal it's an **orphan**: NIC has no SEO or engineering resources for it, and no one knows how to maintain or modify it. So it's quietly **decaying** — losing rankings, traffic, and the monetization it should be capturing every month. `[research: traffic scale + trend, SEO/authority figures, monetization today]`

An authoritative domain with no gardener is a depreciating asset. That's the opportunity.

## 3 · The strategic thesis — three compounding wins

**A. SEO revival — the engine.**
We just rebuilt our own people-search directory's SEO from the studs — technical indexation, sitemap architecture, redirect recovery, content. That same playbook, applied to an *already-authoritative* domain, reverses the decay and grows organic traffic on high-intent property / neighborhood / records queries. HomeFacts has the authority we had to earn; we have the SEO capability it lacks. Fit.

**B. Dual monetization — the payoff for NIC.**
Traffic we grow *for free* pays NIC **twice**:
- **Real-estate & property demand** into NIC's business — leads, listings, foreclosure/property intent, and HomeFacts's own ad inventory. This is NIC's core; it stays 100% NIC's.
- **People-search conversions** we monetize on the records-intent outbound (our $1→membership funnel), with a **transparent revenue share back to NIC** — already attributable by placement (`type` param).

Same traffic, monetized down two lanes that don't compete.

**C. Authority + data flywheel — the moat.**
HomeFacts's property/address graph and IDLookup's people / identity / incarceration / life-events graph are **complementary, not overlapping**. Address → "who lives here" → people search is already the natural flow. Interlinked, cross-referenced, and published on an authoritative domain, the two datasets compound each other's SEO and build a richer, jointly-valuable data asset over time. Neither of us can build this alone.

## 4 · Why us, why now

- **NIC can't maintain it.** Untouched, HomeFacts only decays further. Doing nothing has a cost.
- **We now have the capability** — fresh off our own SEO rebuild — and **aligned incentives**: we're already the traffic partner, and David wins on both sides.
- **Low risk for NIC.** We take the maintenance burden off your hands. You keep ownership, brand, control, and all real-estate value. We prove it on a pilot before anything scales.

## 5 · The model (who does what)

| | IDLookup | NIC / HomeFacts |
|---|---|---|
| **Runs** | SEO · technical maintenance · content · people-search monetization · reporting | Ownership · brand · real-estate/property business + ad inventory |
| **Keeps** | People-search revenue (rev-share to NIC) | 100% of real-estate/property value; all upside on grown traffic |
| **Guardrails** | No brand/structural changes without NIC sign-off; compliance + FCRA maintained | Access to code/CMS; a lightweight pilot agreement |

Revenue is transparent and placement-attributed (we already tag every outbound). Start with a pilot, measure, then scale.

## 6 · Phased plan

- **Phase 0 — Access & baseline (2–4 wks).** Get access to how the site/code is managed; run a full SEO + traffic + monetization audit; set the baseline. *(This is exactly what David is checking on — "how the code is managed.")*
- **Phase 1 — Stop the bleeding (4–8 wks).** Technical SEO triage — indexation, sitemaps, speed, decayed/broken pages — the same recovery we just executed on our own directory.
- **Phase 2 — Grow & integrate (2–4 mo).** Content expansion on high-intent queries; interlink people-search; optimize the outbound → conversion funnel; lift real-estate demand capture.
- **Phase 3 — Compound (ongoing).** Scale content + data interlinking; expand monetization both lanes; report ROI to both sides.

## 7 · What we need to start

Access to **how the code / site is managed** (the exact question already in motion) + a **lightweight pilot agreement**. Low commitment, fast proof.

## 8 · The upside — beyond "validation"

We first framed HomeFacts as a validation/testing channel. It's bigger than that: a **controlled, authoritative growth asset the two of us compound together.** Optionality from here — a template for other orphaned NIC digital assets, a jointly-owned traffic + data moat, and a way to de-risk our own SEO dependency on a young domain. For David, it's rare alignment: the same effort grows HomeFacts *and* the IDLookup investment at once.
