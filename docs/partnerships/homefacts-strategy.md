# HomeFacts × IDLookup — From Affiliate Broker to an Owned Growth Engine

**Initial thoughts for Nations Info Corp (David).** Draft — owner review before sharing. Mirrors the partnership deck (v5). Figures are cited public estimates; the pitch stands on strategic value + economics, not on any ownership tie.

---

## TL;DR

HomeFacts is, underneath the real-estate skin, a **people-search traffic engine** — its #1 organic traffic is sex-offender / person-name queries — and today it **hands that traffic to TruthFinder & Intelius for a thin per-lead affiliate fee** while its own SEO declines. **IDLookup proposes to run HomeFacts's SEO (up to a full rebuild) and convert that traffic through our own people-search funnel** — reversing the decline *and* capturing full customer value instead of a referral sliver. NIC keeps 100% of its real-estate/property monetization (different intent, same pages), takes on **zero cost and zero effort**, and earns a revenue share that beats the affiliate check. The economics stand on their own.

## 1 · The proposal (not yet live)

We've **built** the people-search funnels to receive HomeFacts's records-intent traffic (sex-offender / background / public-records), name+city+state pre-filled — but the switch **isn't on yet.** Turning it on, and running/rebuilding HomeFacts's SEO, is what this conversation is about. The affiliate handoff is **non-exclusive**, so the traffic is HomeFacts's to redirect.

## 2 · The asset — and the reality (verified)

- **A genuinely authoritative domain:** **Moz DA 47**, ~**2,100 referring domains**, **est. 1996 (~30 years old)** — real, hard-won authority (Moz/HypeStat; WHOIS).
- **But declining:** **~135K visits/mo and falling** (SimilarWeb, 2026), ~71% organic. **10.3M indexed pages** on a **2013-era stack (PHP 5.3)** — decaying, not abandoned.
- **Its engine is people-search, not property:** top organic queries are person names + "sex offender"; audience overlaps **Intelius / TruthFinder / TruePeopleSearch**, not Zillow.
- **It monetizes its best intent by brokering it away:** a CAKE redirect hands name/address searches to **TruthFinder & Intelius (both PeopleConnect, non-exclusive)** for a per-lead fee — verified live 2026-07-29, with the searched name passed straight through. The operator on the other end keeps the customer and all lifetime value.

**An authoritative people-search domain, declining, renting its best traffic away for pennies. That's the opportunity.**

## 3 · The #1 lever — own the funnel

Route the same high-intent clicks into **IDLookup's owned funnel + billing** instead of the affiliate broker. NIC shares in **full customer lifetime value**, not a per-lead payout — materially more revenue from the *identical clicks*. We've already built the funnel; the pilot switches it on.

## 4 · The combined value — place × person

HomeFacts knows the **place** (property, address, neighborhood, crime, schools, hazards, sex-offender geography). IDLookup knows the **person** (identity, contact, relatives, first-party incarceration, life-events — plus an owned funnel). The bridge is natural — **address → who lives here → the person.** Combined and interlinked on an authoritative domain: two audiences cross-pollinate, two monetization lanes ride the same pages without competing, and both graphs compound each other's SEO. A jointly-valuable asset neither builds alone.

## 5 · The ambition — rebuild HomeFacts

Not just a bolt-on funnel. Its 2013 stack traps the potential. On the modern infrastructure already running our own SEO directory, we'd rebuild HomeFacts across six workstreams:

1. **Platform** — re-platform off PHP 5.3, **preserving the URL architecture + 301 integrity** so a decade of SEO equity carries over.
2. **SEO** — technical triage (indexation, sitemaps, CWV, redirects) → content quality (de-thin templated pages) → reverse the decline.
3. **Data** — merge the place × person graphs; build address → resident → person; enrich pages across both.
4. **Product / UX** — modernize (mobile, speed, design); embed the owned funnel on records pages; open the display-only address→person vertical.
5. **Monetization** — two lanes: NIC's real-estate/property/mortgage/display (grown, untouched) + owned people-search (rev-share); retire the affiliate broker.
6. **Measurement** — baseline → organic traffic, indexation, conversion, real-estate demand, revenue by lane.

- **Zero cost, zero effort** — untouched, HomeFacts only declines.
- **More money from the same traffic** — LTV share beats the affiliate fee, plus growth.
- **100% of real-estate value stays NIC's**, and a healthier, higher-traffic HomeFacts strengthens NIC's own property-search position (where the real competition — Zillow et al. — is).

## 8 · The model

| | IDLookup | NIC / HomeFacts |
|---|---|---|
| **Runs** | SEO · tech maintenance/rebuild · content · people-search funnel + billing · reporting | Ownership · brand · real-estate / property / mortgage & ad monetization |
| **Keeps** | People-search revenue (rev-share to NIC) | 100% of real-estate value; all upside on grown traffic |
| **Guardrails** | No brand/structural change without NIC sign-off; FCRA/compliance maintained | Access to code/CMS; a lightweight pilot |

**Revenue share: kept open** — structured on the pilot's measured results. The point is the direction, not the split.

**Value logic:** Growth (SEO revival) × Ownership (funnel not affiliate) × Full LTV → materially more revenue on both lanes, at zero cost to NIC. Figures firmed up on the pilot.
