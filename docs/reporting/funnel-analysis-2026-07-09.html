Did the July Redesigns Move Conversion?

Statistical read of the checkout funnel · prepared 2026-07-09 · source: canonical §M17 (verified) + 7/08 daily KPI

Two release rounds sit inside these windows — this matters for attribution:

Round 1 · shipped Jul 3
Landing page + SUP
+ minor changes. Isolated by the After window (7/03–7/07).
Round 2 · shipped Jul 8
Landing v2 + SERP + SUP + PAYMENT
First live day = Yesterday (7/08). The payment page changed here, not in Round 1.

Consequence: the payment page is unchanged in the After window, so any Attempt-step move in 7/03–7/07 cannot be a payment-page effect. Round 2's payment impact only begins to show yesterday.

Bottom line up front
  • One change is statistically significant — and it's a win: Round 1 lifted landing engagement (LP Step 2) 39.5% → 49.4% (p = 0.001).
  • The scary bottom-of-funnel drops are NOT significant. Round 1's Attempt move (23.3% → 11.5%) is p = 0.076; total CVR (2.04% → 0.88%) is p = 0.107 — on 11 vs 5 paid trials. Directional, not conclusive.
  • The payment page is exonerated by timing. It didn't change until Round 2 (7/8); the Attempt dip predates it. And on Round 2's first day, paid Attempt ran 33% — the highest of all three periods.
  • The one large, consistent, actionable signal is thin-match on paid traffic: yesterday 62% of paid searches hit a no-records page that converted at ~0%. That's the real ad-CVR lever — and it's already in flight.

2 · Round 1 (7/3) effects — Before → After · hypotheses & evidence

▲ LP Step 2%  39.5% → 49.4%  SIG · p .001

H1 — The Round-1 landing redesign lifted engagement. (supported)
  • Only significant change; timing coincides exactly with Round 1 (cleaner IA, trust-first hero, tightened wizard step 1).
  • Directionally corroborated by Report-Select (+5.2pt, though NS).
H2 — Traffic/keyword mix improved independently of the page.
  • Not ruled out without keyword data; but Search% (of visitors) barely moved (+2.3, NS), which argues the lift is engagement, not a wholesale intent shift.

Take: bank as a real win; attribute cautiously until keyword mix is confirmed stable.

▼ SUP PII% (signup ÷ search)  27.6% → 20.7%  MARGINAL · p .054

H1 — Round-1 SUP redesign reduced record→signup. (most likely)
  • The "via records" sub-branch fell 40.6% → 31.1% — exactly the page Round 1 touched (headline/bullets/CTA on the SUP).
  • Marginal (p=0.054); yesterday's blended PII% (23%) sits back between the two windows.
H2 — Denominator shift: more traffic to the lower-converting thin-match branch.
  • Thin-match share rose; that branch signs up far less, so blended PII% falls even if each page is unchanged.

Take: real candidate, second priority to thin-match. Re-test the SUP records path directly.

▼ SUP Attempt%  23.3% → 11.5%  MARGINAL · p .076  — "payment problems"

H1 — It is NOT the payment page. (established by timing)
  • The payment page did not change until Round 2 (7/8). This window is 7/03–7/07 — the billing/card form was the pre-redesign version throughout. A payment-page cause is chronologically impossible here.
H2 — Composition: Round-1 changes altered who reaches billing.
  • More top-of-funnel volume (LP Step 2 ↑) can pull in lower-intent visitors who reach billing but abandon the card step — lowering Attempt% without any billing-page change.
H3 — Statistical noise. (cannot be excluded)
  • 17/73 vs 7/61 → z=1.78, p=0.076 — below the bar; one extra conversion after closes most of the gap.

Take: not a payment bug. Confirm composition vs noise with the payment_submit vs Attempt + validation_error pull. No revert warranted.

▼ Total CVR  2.04% → 0.88%  NS · p .107  — the headline number

H1 — Net funnel got worse.
  • Not significant (p=0.107); driven by 11 vs 5 paid trials — far too few to call.
  • Reverses on Round 2's first day (yesterday paid CVR 3.0%).
H2 — Thin-match diverted convertible traffic.
  • More searches ending on a ~0%-converting page mechanically lowers total CVR regardless of page quality.

Take: do not present "CVR halved" as established — it is a non-significant, low-n directional dip.

3 · Round 2 (7/8) effects — After → Yesterday · how the new changes shifted Round 1

Round 2 added Landing v2, SERP, SUP tweaks, and the Payment redesign. One day of paid data — small n, so read as direction, with Wilson 95% CIs.

▲ SUP Attempt%  11.5% (R1) → 33% (R2)  — payment redesign's first read

Round 1 dipped it → Round 2 recovered it above baseline.
H1 — The Round-2 payment redesign did not hurt (may have helped).
  • Payment changed 7/8; the first day shows Attempt at 33% (3/9) — the highest of all three periods, above even the healthy Before (23.3%).
  • 3/9 is tiny (95% CI 12–65%) — spans both prior windows, so "helped" is not yet demonstrable.
H2 — Regression to the mean from a noisy R1 low.
  • If R1's 11.5% was a low-sample fluke (p=0.076), a bounce back toward the low-20s% is expected with or without a redesign.

Take: the payment redesign is not a conversion problem on first evidence. Keep watching as volume builds.

◆ SUP PII%  20.7% (R1) → 23% (R2)

Slight recovery toward baseline.
H1 — Round-2 SUP tweaks partially restored signup.
  • Direction is right, but 9/39 (95% CI 13–38%) overlaps both windows — not distinguishable yet.

◆ Thin-Match View%  14.2% (R1) → 62% (R2 · paid)  — the real lever

Large jump — but paid-specific and partly a one-day spike.
H1 — Paid keywords skew to common names → provider returns no records.
  • Yesterday all 24 thin-match views were in the paid channel; non-paid search went almost entirely to records. Paid clicks disproportionately hit the no-records page.
  • Mechanism understood: common names → IDI teaser TooManyMatches; adding city/age doesn't currently narrow the provider search.
  • Thin-match converted to 0 paid trials yesterday; every conversion came from the records branch.
  • 62% is inflated — thin-match spiked +169% vs day-of-week; steady-state is ~12–14% blended. The direction (paid ≫ non-paid) is the durable part.

Take: this is where ad CVR is actually lost. Two levers already in flight — reduce the rate (provider-side narrowing) and improve the experience (redesigned preview cards + signup A/B shipped 7/8, part of Round 2).

▲ Total CVR  0.88% (R1) → 3.0% paid / 1.2% global (R2)

Up on Round 2's first day.
H1 — Round-2 changes (or normal variance) lifted conversion.
  • Paid 2/67 (95% CI 0.8–10.2%) and global 2/168 (95% CI 0.3–4.2%) are both too wide to confirm a real lift — but neither is consistent with a worsening funnel.

4 · Caveats to disclose (before anyone over-reads this)

  • Sample size / power. Deep-funnel steps carry <20 events per window (paid trials 11 → 5 → 2). At these counts only a very large effect clears p<0.05 — so "not significant" means "under-powered," not "no effect." We should not conclude a break or a fix.
  • Single-day noise. Yesterday's thin-match spike (+169% DoW) shows how volatile daily reads are; Round 2 has one live day. Do not steer roadmap on it yet.
  • Overlapping rounds. Round 2 bundles four changes (landing v2, SERP, SUP, payment) shipped together — their individual effects are not separable from one day of data.
  • Reconstructed intermediate counts. Step-level n's are derived from verified percentages (±1); Attempt / Billing / CVR / Visitors are exact.

5 · What the data supports doing

  • Keep the Round-1 landing redesign. LP Step 2 is the one significant, real gain.
  • Do not revert the payment page. Its changes are Round 2, not the After window; the Attempt dip predates it, and Round 2's first day showed Attempt at 33%. Confirm with the payment_submit vs Attempt + validation_error pull rather than reacting.
  • Prioritize thin-match — it's the real ad-CVR lever. Most paid searches die there at ~0%. Reduce the rate (provider-side narrowing for common names) and improve the experience (already shipping in Round 2).
  • Let volume accumulate before drawing significant conclusions — current windows are directional only, and Round 2 has just one day live.
