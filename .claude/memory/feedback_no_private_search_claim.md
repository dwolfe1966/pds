**NEVER claim a search is private / confidential / anonymous, or that "the person is never notified / never shown."** Applies to all consumer copy — landings, teasers, checkout, ads.

**Why:** we build/offer **WSFY (Who's-Searching-For-You)** — it surfaces searchers *to* the person searched. Any "your search is private, they won't know" claim directly contradicts our own product (and is a promise we can't keep). Competitors (Spokeo/BeenVerified/Instant Checkmate) lean on this "anonymity reassurance"; we deliberately do NOT — it's off the playbook. (Owner, 2026-07-29.)

**How to apply:** for trust signals, use honest alternatives instead — "real results, no fabricated matches," "cancel anytime, no surprise subscriptions," record-scale/security badges. Removed from `/people-search` step 1 + the honest-funnel-spec + competitive-analysis adopt-list. ⚠️ Still LIVE (flagged to owner, not yet fixed) in `PhoneSafetyLandingPage.js` + `PhoneSearchLandingV1Page.js` ("the number's owner is never notified").
